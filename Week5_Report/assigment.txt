This week is Phase 2 of the project. In Phase 1, the Python code acted as both the sender and the receiver of control signals. In Phase 2, those roles are taken by real systems. The MediaPipe hand-tracking application recognizes five hand gestures and publishes them as joint angles. The SysML model (the system of interest, SoI) validates those angles and forwards them. The physical five-joint robotic arm receives the validated signals and moves. The MQTT logic in your Week 4 activity diagram stays the almost same; only the external sender and receiver change.
